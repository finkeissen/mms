## Role ↔ Layer Alignment (Diagnostic)

- **Problem Owner**
  - Allowed layers: 1 (Problem Articulation)
  - Forbidden layers: 2–6
  - Status: **sauber**

- **Claim Author**
  - Allowed layers: 2 (Claim Formation)
  - Forbidden layers: 1, 3–6
  - Status: **sauber**

- **Admissibility Gate (MMS)**
  - Allowed layers: 3 (Structural Admissibility)
  - Forbidden layers: 1–2, 4–6
  - Status: **unklar** (STOP-Befugnis nicht explizit genug benannt)

- **Decision Holder**
  - Allowed layers: 4 (Decision Attribution)
  - Forbidden layers: 1–3, 5–6
  - Status: **sauber**

- **Executor**
  - Allowed layers: 5 (Execution / Application)
  - Forbidden layers: 1–4, 6
  - Status: **sauber**

- **Auditor**
  - Allowed layers: 6 (Audit / Reflection)
  - Forbidden layers: 1–5
  - Status: **sauber**

The Admissibility Gate (MMS) is explicitly authorized to issue **STOP** or **Absence** outcomes.
This authority is limited to structural admissibility checks only and carries no evaluative,
normative, or decision-making power.


## Roles (Descriptive)

- **Problem Owner**  
  Articulates the problem and its boundaries. Carries responsibility for the problem statement.

- **Claim Author**  
  Formulates claims explicitly bound to the articulated problem. Carries responsibility for claim phrasing and scope.

- **Admissibility Gate (MMS)**  
  Performs structural admissibility checks only and may emit STOP / Absence. Carries no evaluative or decision authority.

- **Decision Holder**  
  Makes external commitments and mandates. Carries responsibility for decisions and consequences.

- **Executor**  
  Performs external actions in the world. Carries responsibility for implementation effects.

- **Auditor**  
  Performs ex post inspection. Carries responsibility for traceability and reporting drift.
