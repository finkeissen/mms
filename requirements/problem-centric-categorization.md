# Requirement: Problem-Centric Categorization

## R-001 Ontological Primacy

The fundamental categorization axis of the matrix SHALL be problem instances.

No entry SHALL exist without being anchored to a well-defined problem.

## R-002 Solution Non-Primacy

Solutions SHALL NOT constitute a primary categorization dimension.

Solutions MUST be evaluated relative to a problem, never independently.

## R-003 Model Relativity

Models SHALL be referenced as contextual evaluation frameworks,
but SHALL NOT replace the problem axis.

## R-004 Persistence Under Automation

Any automated system built on top of MMS MUST preserve
problem-centric anchoring of all decision entries.
