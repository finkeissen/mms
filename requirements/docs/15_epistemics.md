# Epistemics

This document defines the epistemic principles
under which the MMS operates.

It does not define goals, domains, or content.
It defines **limits, responsibilities, and stopping conditions**
for any operation carried out within the system.

---

## Limited Cognition

MMS operates under the assumption
that cognition is limited and selective.

No operation has access to:
- complete information
- total context
- continuous representations of reality

All processing is therefore
necessarily partial and constrained.

---

## Reduction Is Unavoidable

Any operation performed by MMS
relies on reduction.

Reduction is not an error
and not a methodological choice.

It is the condition
under which processing is possible at all.

---

## Quantization and Decisions

Certain operations require **quantization**:
the transformation of continuous uncertainty
into discrete states.

Quantization is unavoidable
whenever responsibility must be assumed.

**Diagnosis is a paradigmatic example**:
a forced binary decision under uncertainty
that marks the point where responsibility begins.

Such decisions do not claim truth.
They enable action.

---

## Responsibility

Responsibility arises
where further processing would obscure
the consequences of a decision.

MMS does not remove uncertainty.
It enforces clarity about
where uncertainty is accepted
and where it is cut.

---

## STOP as an Epistemic Boundary

STOP marks the point
at which further processing
would violate the epistemic integrity
of the current quantization.

STOP is not a failure state.

It is the explicit recognition
of a boundary of responsibility.

---

## No Content Authority

MMS does not evaluate content.

It does not decide:
- what is correct
- what is true
- what is optimal

Its authority is limited to:
- enforcing explicit structure
- preventing implicit continuation
- stopping operations
  when epistemic boundaries are reached

---

## Closure

MMS derives its power
not from control,
but from deliberate under-ordering.

It operates by respecting limits,
not by overriding them.

Where cognition ends,
MMS stops.
